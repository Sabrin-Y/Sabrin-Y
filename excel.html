Excel & Data Fundamentals

Bike Sales Data Analysis

Project Overview

As part of my Level 3 Data Technician training, I used Microsoft Excel to analyse a bike sales dataset and explore patterns in revenue and profitability.


I used PivotTables and PivotCharts to summarise the data and create visualisations across different years, countries, product categories and customer age groups.

Skills Learned

Through this project, I developed practical skills in analysing and presenting data using Excel.

Microsoft Excel PivotTables PivotCharts Data Analysis Data Visualisation Data Interpretation

Dataset & Data Used

The dataset contained information relating to bike sales across different years, countries, product categories and customer age groups.


The data included revenue and profit figures, allowing me to explore sales performance and identify patterns across different markets, products and customer groups.

What I Analysed

Revenue & Profit Over Time

I compared annual revenue and profit between 2017 and 2021 to explore changes in business performance over time.

Revenue by Country & Product Category

I compared revenue across different countries and analysed how Accessories, Bikes and Clothing contributed to overall revenue.

Revenue by Customer Age Group

I compared revenue across different customer age groups to identify which demographics generated the most revenue.

What the Data Shows

  • Revenue and profit showed an overall upward trend between 2017 and 2021.
  • Bikes were the largest contributor to revenue across the product categories analysed.
  • The United States generated the highest overall revenue among the countries analysed.
  • Customers aged 35–64 represented the largest revenue-generating customer group.
  • Young Adults aged 25–34 represented the second-largest revenue-generating group.

What I Learned

This project helped me develop my ability to organise, summarise and interpret data using Excel. I learned how PivotTables can be used to explore datasets from different perspectives and how PivotCharts can communicate patterns and trends clearly.


I also developed my ability to interpret data and identify insights that could support business decision-making.
