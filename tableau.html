01 · Spotify Features Analysis

Exploring Music Popularity & Track Characteristics

I used Tableau to analyse a Spotify dataset and explore patterns in music popularity, genres, artists and track characteristics.

Dataset & Data Used

The dataset contained information about Spotify tracks, including genre, popularity, track duration, danceability, acousticness and energy, alongside artist-level information.

What I Analysed

I compared popularity across genres and artists and explored relationships between popularity and track characteristics such as duration and danceability.

What the Data Shows

  • Pop had the highest overall aggregated popularity within the genres shown, followed by Rap and Rock.
  • Drake had the highest popularity value among the artists displayed in the artist analysis.
  • The duration analysis suggests that longer tracks were not automatically more popular, with considerable variation in popularity across track lengths.
  • The dashboard also allowed popularity to be compared against average danceability and examined track characteristics such as acousticness and energy.

02 · Global Health Data Analysis

Exploring Global Health & Population Trends

I used Tableau to explore global health and demographic data, creating visualisations to compare life expectancy, population, cancer measures and relationships between health indicators.

Dataset & Data Used

The dataset contained global health information across different countries and years, including life expectancy, population, gender, cancer measures and BMI-related data.

What I Analysed

I compared life expectancy across continents, explored population by gender, examined cancer measures across countries and analysed changes in life expectancy over time.

What the Data Shows

  • Europe had the highest average life expectancy among the continents shown, while Africa had the lowest.
  • The population by gender visualisation showed a relatively balanced split, with the female population slightly higher than the male population in the displayed data.
  • Cancer measures varied considerably between countries, with China showing the highest values among the countries displayed in the comparison.
  • Life expectancy showed an overall upward trend between 1992 and 2008 across the countries represented in the trend analysis.
  • The BMI and life expectancy visualisation allowed the relationship between the two measures to be explored across selected countries and demographic groups.
